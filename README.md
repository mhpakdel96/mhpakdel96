## About Me

I'm a Biotechnology PhD candidate at the **National Institute of Genetic Engineering and Biotechnology (NIGEB)**, currently a **Visiting Scholar at ZHAW School of Life Sciences and Facility Management** (Switzerland). My work sits at the intersection of population genomics, evolutionary biology, and computational structural biology, applied to long-lived, clonally propagated crops and beyond.

I led the full bioinformatic pipeline for a genome-scale study of the Iranian olive (*Olea europaea*) collection — 215 global accessions — resolving a novel evolutionary lineage and mapping the genetic basis of oil-quality traits via GWAS. I also build and maintain reproducible, HPC-scale (SLURM) analysis pipelines from raw sequencing reads through population structure, phylogenomics, structural-variant discovery, and multi-omic integration.

## Current Focus

- 🌿 **Population & comparative genomics** of *Olea europaea* — WGS variant discovery, phylogenomics, selection-sweep and LD-decay analysis, cross-species synteny
- 🧬 **GWAS & trait mapping** — mixed-model association, pathway enrichment, candidate-gene prioritization
- 🧪 **Structural biology / molecular dynamics** — protein stability simulations (GROMACS/CHARMM36m), extending computational skills beyond genomics into biophysical characterization

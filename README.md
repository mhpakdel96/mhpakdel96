## About Me

I'm a Biotechnology PhD candidate at the **National Institute of Genetic Engineering and Biotechnology (NIGEB)** and current **Visiting Scholar at ZHAW School of Life Sciences and Facility Management** (Switzerland). I specialize in large-scale genomic data analysis, variant discovery, and multi-omic data integration, with end-to-end computational workflows built in Python, R, and Bash on SLURM-based HPC clusters.

My flagship project is a genome-scale population study of the Iranian olive (*Olea europaea*) collection — 215 global accessions — resolving a novel evolutionary lineage and mapping the genetic basis of oil-quality traits via GWAS. Alongside this, I work on **host–microbiome and probiotic genomics**, characterizing probiotic bacteria at the whole-genome level and studying host gene-regulatory responses to bacterial pathogens in livestock. More recently, I've also applied computational structural biology methods (molecular dynamics) to protein stability questions.

I'm interested in research and collaboration opportunities spanning plant/crop population genomics, host–microbiome interactions, and computational systems biology.

## Current Focus

- 🌿 **Population & comparative genomics** of *Olea europaea* — WGS variant discovery, phylogenomics, selection-sweep and LD-decay analysis, cross-species synteny
- 🧬 **GWAS & trait mapping** — mixed-model association, pathway enrichment, candidate-gene prioritization
- 🦠 **Host–microbiome & probiotic genomics** — whole-genome and functional characterization of probiotic bacteria; gene-regulatory response to mastitis-associated pathogens in livestock
- 🧪 **Structural biology / molecular dynamics** — protein heme- and ligand-binding stability simulations (GROMACS/CHARMM36m)
